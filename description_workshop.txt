[h1]TerraStorage Overflow[/h1]

An addon for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3687137546]Requisition[/url]. Instead of leaving items on the ground or manually clicking through menus, 
this mod turns your Remote Terminal into a smart vacuum for your storage network. Similar to "Void Bag" functionality, but with a few extra features.

A new tab has been added to the terminal UI to easily sell duplicate equipment (inspired by Magic Storage).

I've also added sorting buttons for Clicker Class (Clicker Damager) and Capture Disc Class (CaptureDamage), feel free to request support for any other damage type. 
These buttons will only appear if the corresponding mods are installed.

[h2]🛠 Features[/h2]

[list]
[*] [b]Smart Overflow:[/b] Automatically redirects picked-up items to your storage disks if your inventory is full, similar to what the void bag does. 
[*] [b]Improved Interaction:[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Shift-Click Redirect:[/b] Shift-clicking from chests will bypass your inventory and go straight to the network if you're out of space.
[*] [b]Loot All Integration:[/b] Hit "Loot All" on a chest and watch the excess fly directly into your network if you're out of space.
[/list]
[*] [b]Live Tooltips:[/b] Hover over a remote to see its bound coordinates and a real-time capacity readout (Used / Total).
[*] [b]Multiplayer Optimized:[/b] Uses a custom buffering system to batch item deposits. (untested, I have no friends).
[/list]
[list]
[*] [b]Extra Terminal Tab:[/b] A new tab in the terminal UI to easily sell duplicate equipment (inspired by Magic Storage).
[/list]
