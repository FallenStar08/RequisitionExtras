[h1]Requisition Extras[/h1]

A small addon for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3687137546][b]Requisition[/b][/url]. Instead of leaving items on the ground or manually clicking through menus, this mod turns your Remote Terminal into a smart vacuum for your storage network.

[h2]🛠 Features[/h2]

[i] [b]Smart Overflow:[/b] Automatically redirects picked-up items to your storage disks if your inventory is full, similar to what the void bag does. 
[/i] [b]Multi-Network Support:[/b] The mod intelligently cycles through all bound networks until the item finds a home.
[i] [b]Improved Interaction:[/b]
    [/i] [b]Shift-Click Redirect:[/b] Shift-clicking from chests will bypass your inventory and go straight to the network if you're out of space.
    [i] [b]Loot All Integration:[/b] Hit "Loot All" on a chest and watch the excess fly directly into your network if you're out of space.
[/i] [b]Live Tooltips:[/b] Hover over a remote to see its bound coordinates and a real-time capacity readout (Used / Total).
[i] [b]Custom Tab:[/b] A new "Extras" Tab which contains a fancy interface to easily sell duplicate equipment (inspired by Magic Storage's "Sell Duplicates" feature)
[/i] [b]A New Button in the Crafting Core Interface:[/b] A new button allows auto-populating the slots with all stations that would fulfill new conditions for crafting from the storage network. (This also supports swapping a station if a new one is found that would fulfill the same condition and more)
[i] [b]Multiplayer Optimized:[/b] Uses a custom buffering system to batch item deposits. (Untested in multiplayer, but should work fine.)
