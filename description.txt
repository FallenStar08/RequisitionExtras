A Quality of Life expansion for the Requisition mod.

FEATURES:
- Smart Overflow: Items go to storage if your inventory is full (Void Bag style).
- Multiple Networks: Automatically cycles through all bound remotes in your inventory. 
- Shift-Click Redirect: Shift-clicking from chests bypasses full inventories.
- Loot All Support: Excess items from chests fly directly into your disks.
- Live Tooltips: See disk capacity and coordinates on the item's tooltip.
- Extra tab in the terminal UI to easilly sell duplicate equipment (inspired by magic storage)
- Multiplayer Optimized: Batches deposits to prevent server lag. (Untested)

Requires Requisition (Formerly known as TerraStorage) (Base Mod) to function.
